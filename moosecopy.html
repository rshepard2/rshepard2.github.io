Explore Music in the Bay Area

The Bay Area is perhaps best known for technology and entrepreneurship; art plays only a small role in its reputation. Despite that, a vibrant, multicultural music scene flourishes here, fueled by people of many ethnicities, backgrounds, and interests.

What does music look like in the area? What influences artists? What challenges do they face?

We have interviewed a variety of people involved in Bay Area music. You can hear more about their stories by clicking on the markers to the left.

We've also included a layer of demographic information to contextualize the changing landscape of the Bay Area, as well as a layer containing information on arts nonprofits in the Bay. You can explore them by clicking the dropdowns below.

About this project
This project was a collaborative effort between Ryan Eberhardt, Sonja Johnson-Yu, Christopher Swenson, Francisco Izaguirre, and the Anna Schultz's course Music Ethnography of the Bay Area. The project began through a collaboration between Daniel Murray of Stanford's CSRE department, Molly Dow, the special projects advisor of PeaceTones, and Anna Schultz of Stanford's music department. After the first prototype was generated by students in Anna Schultz's 2016 class, the Haas Center funded a summer of student community-based research to assess the needs of the Bay Area arts ecosystem. This map's focus incorporates the input of a number of stakeholders, including individual artists, organizations, advocates, and funders. It offers both a birds-eye view of arts in the bay area, as well as an on-the-ground documentary glimpse of specific musicians and organizations. This project is intended to be an explorative and informative tool for understanding the Bay Area arts ecosystem.

Monique Martinez

Monique Martinez is a Bay Area R&B singer who has performed in a variety of venues including multiple appearances as part of the Super Bowl series that takes place in SF every year. Her recent collaborations with Ivan Reyes and Templo Audio have shifted in an EDM direction. Monique aspires to continue developing her career as an independent artist and is looking forward to releasing a new EP in the coming months.
